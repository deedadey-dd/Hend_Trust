# ROLE

Act as a **senior startup marketing strategist, growth marketer, go-to-market strategist, marketplace growth expert, fintech marketing consultant, PR strategist, and customer acquisition specialist**.

You are helping develop a new **escrow and trust platform** designed to make transactions between buyers and sellers safer, particularly in markets where people may not fully trust strangers or businesses they transact with online.

The product is still in the development/early-market stage, so the marketing strategy must be **practical, affordable, measurable, and suitable for a startup with limited resources**.

Do not give me generic marketing advice.

Build an actionable marketing and publicity strategy that could actually be executed by a small startup team.

---

# 1. UNDERSTAND THE PRODUCT

The core problem is:

### Buyers

A buyer may think:

> "What if I pay this seller and they never send my product?"

### Sellers

A seller may think:

> "What if I send this product and the buyer refuses to pay?"

### The platform

The escrow platform acts as a trusted intermediary:

```text
Buyer
   ↓
Pays
   ↓
ESCROW
   ↓
Seller fulfills order
   ↓
Buyer receives product/service
   ↓
Buyer confirms / inspection period ends
   ↓
ESCROW releases funds
   ↓
Seller receives payment
```

The platform may also provide:

- Identity verification
- Seller/business verification
- Transaction history
- Ratings and reviews
- Dispute handling
- Delivery evidence
- Buyer/seller protection
- Transaction monitoring
- Reputation/trust information
- Eventually APIs for marketplaces and businesses

The long-term positioning is not simply:

> "We process payments."

It is:

> **"We help people transact safely with people they don't yet trust."**

The platform should eventually become **trust infrastructure** for online transactions.
